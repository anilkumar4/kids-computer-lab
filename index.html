About Kids Computer Lab

This is a safe, ad-free educational space designed to introduce foundational computer literacy.

  • Little Explorers (UKG): Focuses on recognizing a computer, mouse/touchpad basics, and basic safety. Needs adult supervision.
  • Tech Champions (Class 4): CBSE-friendly hardware concepts, typing basics, and digital citizenship. Can be done independently.

🔒 Privacy & Data

This application is completely private by design:

  • No accounts required. Progress is saved directly to your device's web browser (localStorage).
  • No tracking. We do not collect names, emails, usage data, or analytics.
  • No ads or external links in the child-facing areas.

Note: If you clear your browser history/data, progress will be lost.
